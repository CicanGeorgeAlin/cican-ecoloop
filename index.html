The Combined Return Method

The philosophy and practical manual behind CICAN ECOLOOP.

OUR PRINCIPLE
24 HOURS. ONE RESOURCE WE ALL SHARE.

Don't just spend your time. Invest it.

We all have the same 24 hours. We cannot create more time, but we can become more intentional about how we use it. CICAN ECOLOOP explores how ordinary activities can be combined to create more value from the same block of time.

01 · Where the idea began

Some surveys can take 20 or 30 minutes and may reward only €0.25. That raises a simple question: what else could those same minutes achieve? Could a walk to find an eligible bottle and return it to a recycling station produce another €0.25?

Then came the next thought: why choose between surveys and recycling if both can be combined during one journey?

02 · The backwards-thinking approach

Instead of staying comfortable and putting off a walk to look for bottles, use the motivation to complete surveys as a reason to get outside. Walk with purpose, stay observant, collect eligible containers safely, and continue your survey whenever it is safe and practical.

Recycling becomes part of a journey you already wanted to make — not a separate chore competing for your time.

03 · One period of time, two opportunities

Imagine a ten-minute journey. You walk five minutes from Point A and find an eligible bottle. You collect it safely, then walk another five minutes to a recycling station. During that same journey, you complete a survey.

04 · Two different kinds of return

Surveys: rewards vary with availability, eligibility, completion, time and payment status. An attempted or rejected survey must not be recorded as money earned.

Recycling: eligible containers have a defined deposit value, but finding them, travelling to a return point and using the machine take variable amounts of time. Record the actual refund, not an assumed one.

05 · Measure honestly

  • Record the full journey time, including searching and walking.
  • Record survey attempts, outcomes, duration and actual rewards.
  • Record eligible containers collected and actually returned.
  • Record the actual refund and, where useful, distance and effort.
  • Count overlapping minutes only once.
  • Do not assume this method is always better; let real data show the result.

06 · Calculate the combined return

Add actual survey rewards that have been earned to actual deposit refunds received. Divide by the total elapsed time for the journey, counting simultaneous activities as one shared period.

Combined hourly return
Actual survey earnings + actual refunds
÷ total elapsed journey hours

Keep pending survey rewards separate. Do not count theoretical container equivalents as real refunds or as containers recycled.

07 · A positive contribution

Collecting eligible discarded containers safely can help keep litter out of public spaces and return recyclable material to the recovery system. The purpose is not only financial. It is also about environmental responsibility and contributing to a cleaner community.

08 · Safety comes first

Never use your phone while crossing roads, cycling, navigating hazards or handling containers. Stop in a safe place before doing a survey. Do not collect broken, sharp, contaminated or otherwise unsafe items. Only return containers accepted by the deposit scheme.
